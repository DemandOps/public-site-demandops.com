Our thesis

Home projects stall for want of a clear plan.

Most home improvements that never happen stall at the start. The ambition arrives as one big, vague job that is hard to agree on, price or afford. We help households turn it into a clear specification and a sequence of steps, kept with the property so they can phase the work over time and pick up where they left off.

Specify it.

Inspiration is easy to find. What stalls a project is not knowing exactly what should change in your own home, how big it is and roughly what it costs. A clear specification gives the household something to agree on.

Sequence it.

A project priced as one large job often never starts. Split into steps in a sensible order, the same ambition becomes affordable, one step at a time.

Make it doable.

A vague request is hard to price and easy to decline. A well-defined step can be done by whoever suits it: a professional, a handy local helper or the owner.

For everyone.

A personal design and a considered plan used to need a paid architect. AI can now understand a photo of your own home, change only what you ask for and help turn the result into practical steps.

How it works

In TuinPlan, it starts with your own garden.

Work in your own order. Everything stays with your garden, ready for the next decision.

  1. 01

    Capture your garden

    Enter your address. Your plot, house and sunlight are already there. Add a few photos of how it looks today.

  2. 02

    Find your style

    Choose a style you love and say what should stay and what can change.

  3. 03

    Create a Vision Card

    See your own garden transformed, then try other directions.

  4. 04

    Ask what friends think

    Share your Vision Card with family and friends. They can react without installing anything.

  5. 05

    Keep and develop your design

    Refine the layout on a computer, or save a Garden Spec with measurements and an estimate to share as a PDF.
